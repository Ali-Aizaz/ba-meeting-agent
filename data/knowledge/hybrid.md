# Hybrid

A hybrid car (HEV) has an internal combustion engine and an electric motor. The motor helps the car move at low speed and in city traffic. The engine takes over more of the work on the highway. The battery is charged by the engine and by regenerative braking. A plain full hybrid is not plugged in.

## Regenerative braking

Regenerative braking uses the electric motor as a generator when the car slows down or coasts, capturing kinetic energy to recharge the hybrid or EV battery and reducing brake pad wear.

## Mild hybrid vs full hybrid

A mild hybrid (MHEV) uses a small battery (often 48V) and starter-generator to assist the engine and smooth stop-start operation, but cannot drive on electric power alone. A full hybrid (HEV) can propel the car short distances at low speed on electric power alone.

## Plug-in hybrid

A plug-in hybrid (PHEV) has a larger battery and an external charge port. It can drive a moderate distance on electricity alone, then switch to hybrid operation using the engine.
